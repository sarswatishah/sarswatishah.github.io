Research profile

Mathematics for nonlinear transport

My research combines mathematical analysis, structure-preserving numerical methods, scientific computing, and data-driven modeling. I study complex transport phenomena arising in multiphase and geophysical flows, energy systems, and nonlinear optics.

I am currently a postdoctoral research fellow in the Department of Mathematical Sciences and the Center for Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence at George Mason University, working with Prof. Harbir Antil .
